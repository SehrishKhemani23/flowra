About

About FLOWRA AI

A student-led business automation initiative founded by Sehrish Khemani and Divya Kingrani.

Who We Are

FLOWRA AI is a student-led initiative exploring how AI-powered automation can help small businesses simplify operations and improve customer communication. We are at prototype stage and building in the open.

Our Mission

To make intelligent automation accessible and practical for small businesses.

Our Vision

To create smarter business experiences through accessible AI-powered technology.

What We Do

We design and prototype customer-support automation, inquiry and order handling, and owner dashboards for restaurants, retailers and other small businesses.
